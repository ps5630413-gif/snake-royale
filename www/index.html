    HOW TO PLAY

    • Move — drag the virtual joystick, or swipe anywhere on screen.
    • Boost — hold the BOOST button (costs mass, so stay fed).
    • Grow — eat glowing energy dots and the remains of dead snakes.
    • Coins — grab golden coins to spend in the Skins Shop.
    • Permadeath — touching another snake's body or the arena wall kills you instantly.
    • Trap — bigger bots will try to cut you off. Circle them and let them crash into you.
    • Revive — after dying you can watch one rewarded video to continue with your score.

    Keyboard (desktop testing): arrows / WASD to steer, SPACE to boost, ESC to pause.
